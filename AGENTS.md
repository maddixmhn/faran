# Faran Repository Rules

- Keep the first release local and mock-only; do not enable external services or publish content.
- Keep schedules disabled unless explicitly configured.
- Preserve synthetic-only education, HR, and finance data.
- Before adding an orchestration subsystem, document reuse candidates and verify their source, licence, compatibility, maintenance, and security.
- See `docs/ROADMAP.md` for scope and acceptance criteria.
